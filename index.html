The basics

What Is Auto Glass Repair in Bowie?

Auto glass repair in Bowie covers every piece of glass on a car, truck or SUV: filling a rock chip with resin, replacing a cracked windshield, putting in a new side or back window, and re-aiming the safety camera that looks through the windshield. Commuters on US-50, US-301 and Route 197 pick up more stone chips than most, and Maryland winters turn small chips into long cracks.

The windshield is a safety part, not just a window. It is bonded to the body with urethane, it helps the roof hold up in a rollover and it gives the passenger airbag something to push against, so how it is installed matters as much as the glass itself.

Signs to watch for

When Do You Need Windshield Repair or Replacement?

Most calls start with one of these moments. If one of them matches your car, the glass needs attention soon.

A stone hit on the highway

Gravel and salt trucks on Route 50 and Route 3 throw debris. A fresh chip can usually still be filled.

A chip that grew overnight

Freezing nights and a hot defroster in the morning are the most common way a chip becomes a crack.

A crack in your line of sight

Damage right in front of the driver distorts the view and usually means new glass.

A broken side or back window

A break-in or a stray ball leaves the car open to rain, cold and theft until the glass is replaced.

Plow and scraper damage

Chunks of ice off other cars and hard scraping in winter chip and scratch glass.

A warning light after new glass

A lane-keep or collision alert after a windshield job means the camera was not calibrated.

After the glass

What Happens to Your Tint When a Window Is Replaced

A lot of the side and back window jobs we do in Bowie come from break-ins, a regulator that gives out on a cold morning, or a rock kicked up on Route 50. If that window had aftermarket film on it, the film goes in the trash with the broken glass. New replacement glass is clear unless your vehicle had factory privacy glass in the rear, so the car can look mismatched until the window is tinted again.

Maryland keeps tint lighter than many states. On a passenger car, the front side, back side and rear windows all have to let in at least 35 percent of outside light, and the windshield can only have film in the top five inches. SUVs, vans and pickups have more freedom behind the driver. If an inspection or a traffic stop is on your mind, ask a tint shop to meter the glass before any film goes on.

We replace the glass and leave the film to the specialists. Keep a new door window rolled up for a day and give a new windshield a couple of days to cure first. When you start looking into window tinting, ask about heat rejection, how the film holds up through a Maryland summer, and what the warranty covers if it bubbles or peels.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Auto Glass in Bowie

Does insurance pay for a new windshield in Maryland?

Glass damage is usually covered under the comprehensive part of an auto policy. Maryland does not have a law that waives the deductible for windshields, so what you pay depends on your deductible and whether your policy adds full glass coverage. Many insurers waive the deductible for a chip repair, so fixing a chip early often costs nothing.

Can a windshield chip be repaired instead of replaced?

Often it can. A chip smaller than a quarter, or a single crack shorter than about six inches, can usually be filled with resin if it is not at the edge of the glass or right in front of the driver. Tell us the size and where it sits and we will say which fix fits.

Why do windshield cracks spread so fast in the winter?

A chip is a weak spot in the outer layer of glass. On a cold Bowie morning, blasting hot defrost air or pouring warm water on an icy windshield makes the glass expand unevenly, and that stress runs the chip into a crack. Water that seeps into the chip and freezes overnight pushes it open too.

Do you come to my home or office?

Yes. Bowie Auto Glass Repair is a mobile service, so the technician works in your driveway, a townhouse lot or the parking lot at your job. The address on this site is a service office, not a shop where you drop the car off.

How long does a windshield replacement take?

The install takes about an hour. After that the urethane adhesive needs one to three hours before the car is safe to drive, depending on the product and the temperature. A chip repair is much quicker, usually about 30 minutes.

Can you replace a windshield when it is cold or raining?

The glass and the frame have to be dry and the adhesive needs to be within its working temperature to bond. In rain, snow or hard cold we work in a garage or a covered parking deck, or move the appointment to a better day.

Does my car need camera calibration after a new windshield?

If the vehicle has lane-keep assist, automatic emergency braking or adaptive cruise control, the camera behind the rearview mirror usually needs to be calibrated after the glass is replaced. We check for it when we give the quote.
