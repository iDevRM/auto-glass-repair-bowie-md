What Does Windshield Replacement Involve?

Windshield replacement starts with covers over the hood, fenders and dash. The technician cuts the old glass free from its urethane bond and trims the old adhesive down to an even layer. Any bare metal on the frame is primed so rust cannot start under the new seal. Fresh urethane goes down, the new windshield is set and centered, and the mirror, sensors and moldings go back on. The install takes about an hour, plus cure time.

When Do You Need Windshield Replacement?

A crack longer than a dollar bill

Long cracks cannot be stopped with resin and keep running.

Damage in the driver’s view

Resin leaves a faint mark, so breaks in front of the driver need new glass.

A crack at the edge

Edge cracks weaken the bond between glass and frame and spread fast.

Several chips at once

Three or more chips, often after a winter of highway driving, add up to weak glass.

A leak at the top corners

Water inside after a storm often points to a failed seal.

A failed inspection

A Maryland State Inspection can fail a car for a crack in the driver’s view.

Why Do Windshield Cracks Spread in Maryland Winters?

A windshield is two layers of glass with a clear plastic layer in between, and a stone chip breaks the outer layer. Each time the glass heats up and cools down it expands and shrinks, which pulls at the ends of the break. On a freezing morning in Bowie, hot defrost air on cold glass is the classic trigger. Water that gets into a chip and freezes overnight also pushes the break wider.

Frequently Asked Questions

How soon can I drive after a new windshield in Bowie?

Most urethane adhesives reach safe drive-away strength in one to three hours, depending on the product and the temperature. Cure takes longer in cold weather, and the technician gives you the exact time before leaving. Driving too soon can let the glass shift or weaken the bond the airbag relies on.

Is OEM glass better than aftermarket glass?

OEM glass is made by the same supplier that built your original windshield. Good aftermarket glass meets the same federal safety rules, but on cars with cameras or heads-up displays OEM glass can make calibration easier. We tell you which options fit your vehicle and your insurance.

When can I wash my car after a windshield replacement?

Wait at least a day before a car wash, and keep pressure washers away from the edges of the glass for a couple of days so the new seal is not disturbed.

Do I need ADAS calibration with a new windshield?

If there is a camera behind the rearview mirror, the answer is usually yes. The camera has to be aimed again for the new glass so lane-keep and emergency braking work as designed. We confirm it for your vehicle with the quote.
